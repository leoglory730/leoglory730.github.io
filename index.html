01 / Research logic

Policy does not act on a passive system.

Its effects are mediated by the strategic responses of airlines, rail operators, ports, infrastructure providers, fuel suppliers, financiers, platforms and governments.

My work connects transportation economics and operations management to explain the full path from a policy or market shock to strategy, interaction, equilibrium and system performance.

Current frontier

Aviation decarbonization as a strategic market transition.

Sustainable aviation fuel is not only a technology choice. It is a coordination problem spanning airline competition, mandates and subsidies, airport logistics, production investment, finance and international climate policy.

Across a growing portfolio of SAF articles and projects, I examine how mandates, subsidies, airline business models, CORSIA design, production incentives and airport logistics interact. This work links formal models to policy evaluation, conference presentations and public explanation—building a coherent research programme from mechanism to implementation.
