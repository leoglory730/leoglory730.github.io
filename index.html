02 / How impact is built

Impact begins with the mechanism.

Transport policies rarely act directly. Their effects are mediated by how airlines, rail operators, ports, infrastructure providers, suppliers, financiers and governments change their strategies.

My research makes that response visible, tests its consequences and then traces whether the resulting evidence is recognised, used or carried into wider debate.

01

Identify the mechanism

Explain how competition, cooperation, capacity, uncertainty and policy jointly determine transport outcomes.

02

Build decision evidence

Translate formal analysis and empirical evaluation into results that can inform policy, operations and investment.

03

Document the uptake

Distinguish scholarly influence, formal analytical use, institutional engagement and public communication.

Evidence beyond publication

Where the research has travelled.

The strongest impact case rests on an evidence trail: independent studies that recognise the work, formal documents that use it, institutions that convene around it and public channels that make the findings accessible.

These records demonstrate scholarly uptake, analytical use and decision-facing reach. They should not be read as causal proof that a particular government or firm changed a decision because of one study.
