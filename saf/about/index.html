Research identity

Economics for implementable transitions.

Changmin Jiang studies how transport markets respond to policy, technology and institutional design. His aviation work combines game theory, policy modelling, empirical analysis and operational detail to examine choices made by governments, airlines, airports and fuel suppliers.

SAF is a defining strand of this agenda: from carbon tax versus quota and carrier competition to airport subsidy design, infrastructure ownership, CORSIA, e-fuels, flexible compliance and regional pathway selection. The result is a coherent programme aimed at moving aviation decarbonisation from general ambition to workable rules.
