Impact theme · Sea + ports

Resilient maritime systems begin with the incentives behind capacity, coordination and adaptation.

My maritime research connects port operations and shipping strategy to congestion, hinterland transport, Arctic risk, green investment and climate resilience.

Core contribution

Linking operational decisions to system resilience.

A port’s capacity choice or a carrier’s alliance decision propagates across routes, congestion, hinterland modes and environmental performance.

By modelling those links explicitly, the work supports more realistic infrastructure investment, coordination and climate-adaptation decisions across globally connected maritime systems.

01

Port capacity & congestion

Clarifies strategic investment when ports and shipping alliances affect one another.

02

Green hinterland transport

Designs incentives for cleaner port-to-inland logistics and intermodal coordination.

03

Climate adaptation

Evaluates when demonstrators, timing and uncertainty change the value of infrastructure adaptation.

04

Arctic & disruption risk

Extends transport economics to oil-spill exposure, new routes and fragile operating environments.
