Impact theme · Air + rail

Competition becomes integration when networks, capacity and welfare are considered together.

My air–rail programme treats modes not as isolated technologies but as strategic systems whose networks, prices, frequencies and infrastructure constraints interact.

Core contribution

Making modal interaction endogenous.

High-speed rail changes more than airline traffic. It changes network coverage, scheduling discipline, airport capacity use and the value of cooperation.

This work maps the chain from modal entry and capacity constraints to operator strategy, market equilibrium, passenger welfare and emissions—providing a framework for evaluating competition and partnership together.

01

Network choice

Explains why airlines adjust route coverage and hub strategies when high-speed rail enters.

02

Operational performance

Shows how rail competition can alter airline on-time performance and service incentives.

03

Partnership design

Identifies when air–rail cooperation improves welfare under market and airport-capacity constraints.

04

Climate consequences

Evaluates emissions through system-wide modal interaction rather than simple passenger substitution.
