About

Changmin Jiang

Associate Professor in the Department of Logistics and Maritime Studies at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

My work sits at the intersection of transportation economics, industrial organization and operations management. I develop theory and empirical evidence to explain how transport firms respond to competition, policy and uncertainty—and how those responses reshape networks, welfare, emissions and market structure.

Mentoring

Building an independent research community.

Supervision spans PhD, postdoctoral and master’s researchers in transportation economics, supply chain management and policy. Student co-authorship is central to the research programme, from sustainable aviation fuel and airport strategy to low-altitude mobility, shipping and infrastructure resilience.

Teaching experience includes air transport regulatory policy, shipping economics and markets, airline business, transport economics, purchasing and supply management, and supply chain operations.
